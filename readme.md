# MedSupply Intelligence

### AI-Powered Healthcare Inventory & Procurement Intelligence Platform

MedSupply Intelligence is a healthcare supply-chain intelligence platform designed to help hospitals and pharmacies monitor medicine inventory, understand consumption patterns, predict future demand, identify supply risks, and generate actionable procurement recommendations.

The system connects **supplier invoices, inventory transactions, pharmacy billing, demand forecasting, risk detection, and procurement intelligence** into a single operational workflow.

---

## Problem

Healthcare facilities can face serious operational problems when inventory is managed reactively:

- Critical medicines may run out unexpectedly.
- Excess inventory can expire before use.
- Sudden changes in consumption may go unnoticed.
- Procurement decisions may depend on manual calculations.
- Supplier lead times can make shortages difficult to recover from.
- Inventory, billing, and procurement data may exist in disconnected systems.

These problems can lead to:

**Stockouts → Emergency Procurement → Higher Costs → Operational Risk**

MedSupply Intelligence addresses this by converting operational inventory data into predictive and actionable intelligence.
